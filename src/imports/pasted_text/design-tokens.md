Do you eat candies, chocolates, or sweet gummies every day? (No / Only sometimes / Yes, a lot!)

Do you drink sweet juices or sodas with your meals? (No, just water / Sometimes / Yes, often)

Does a grown-up help you brush your teeth? (Yes, always / Sometimes / I brush all by myself)

Do you use a soft brush made just for kids? (Yes / No / I don't know)

Is brushing your teeth fun for you? (Yes, I love it! / It is okay / No, I don't like it)

Do your gums bleed when you brush or floss? (Yes, almost every time / Only sometimes / No, never)

Do your gums look red, puffy, or feel sore? (Yes, they look red and sore / A little bit / No, they look healthy and pink)

How many times a day do you brush your teeth? (2 times or more / 1 time / I sometimes skip days)

Do you use dental floss or a tiny brush to clean between your teeth? (Yes, every day / Only sometimes / No, never)

Does your breath often smell bad, even right after brushing? (Yes / Sometimes / No, rarely)

Do you drink soda, sweet tea, or coffee with sugar during the day? (Yes, multiple times / Only once or twice / No, rarely)

Have you visited a dentist for a professional cleanup in the last year? (Yes / No, it has been longer / I can't remember)
